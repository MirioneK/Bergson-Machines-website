Buying a mini excavator is one of the most common decisions faced by construction companies, installation contractors, and people carrying out earthworks. Many choose renting at first, but in practice it often turns out very quickly that owning your own machine is a much more cost-effective solution.

In this article, we will show how much mini excavator rental costs, how much it costs to buy one, and when the investment starts to pay for itself.

## How much does it cost to rent a mini excavator?

In Poland, the cost of renting a mini excavator is most often:

- PLN 200-400 per day without an operator
- PLN 500-900 per day with an operator

In practice, this means that with regular work:

- 10 working days = PLN 2,000-4,000
- 20 working days = PLN 4,000-8,000
- 2-3 months of work = even PLN 10,000-20,000

In addition, you often need to account for:

- transport costs,
- limited equipment availability,
- lack of flexibility in scheduling.

## How much does it cost to buy a mini excavator?

Buying a mini excavator typically means spending:

- from about PLN 19,999 net for basic 1-ton models,
- from PLN 24,999 net for 1.2-ton models,
- PLN 30,000-40,000 net for versions with a cabin or a Kubota engine.

This is a one-time cost, which can also be spread out through installments or leasing.

## When does a mini excavator pay for itself?

This is the most important question.

Let’s assume a simple scenario:

- rental cost: PLN 300 per day on average,
- work schedule: 15 days per month,
- monthly rental cost = PLN 4,500.

In that case, after 5 months you spend PLN 22,500, which is roughly the price of your own mini excavator.

In practice, this means that after one season, the machine starts generating profit for itself. Every additional month is pure savings compared to renting.

## Renting vs buying - comparison

### Renting

Advantages:

- no large upfront expense,
- no servicing responsibilities on your side.

Disadvantages:

- high long-term costs,
- limited availability during the season,
- dependence on rental companies.

### Buying

Disadvantage:

- initial investment cost.

Advantages:

- full equipment availability,
- ability to work whenever you want,
- no rental fees,
- the machine works for your profit.

## Mini excavator leasing - a middle-ground solution

If you do not want to commit all your cash at once, you can choose leasing.

In many cases, the monthly lease payment is similar to the cost of renting. The difference is that with leasing, you are building your own asset, while with renting the money simply disappears.

Additionally, leasing offers:

- tax benefits,
- VAT deduction possibilities,
- equipment depreciation.

## Who benefits most from buying a mini excavator?

Buying makes sense if:

- you perform work regularly, even just a few days per month,
- you run a construction, paving, or installation business,
- you want to become independent from rental companies,
- you plan to grow your business.

If you use a mini excavator more than just a few times a month, buying almost always wins.

## Summary - is buying a mini excavator worth it?

Renting only makes sense for occasional work. However, if you work regularly, your own mini excavator pays for itself very quickly and starts generating real profit.

That is why more and more companies choose to buy instead of rent.
