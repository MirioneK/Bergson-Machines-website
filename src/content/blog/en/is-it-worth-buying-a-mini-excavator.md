Buying a mini excavator is one of the most common decisions faced by construction companies, installation contractors, and people carrying out earthworks. Many people choose renting at first, but in practice it quickly turns out that owning your own machine is a much more cost-effective solution.

In this article, we will show how much mini excavator rental costs, how much the purchase costs, and when the investment starts to pay off.

## How Much Does It Cost to Rent a Mini Excavator?

In Poland, the cost of renting a mini excavator most often ranges from:

- PLN 200–400 per day without an operator
- PLN 500–900 per day with an operator

In practice, this means that with regular work:

- 10 working days = PLN 2,000–4,000
- 20 working days = PLN 4,000–8,000
- 2–3 months of work = even PLN 10,000–20,000

On top of that, there are often:

- transport costs,
- limited equipment availability,
- lack of flexibility in scheduling.

## How Much Does a Mini Excavator Cost?

Buying a mini excavator means an expense of roughly:

- from around PLN 19,999 net — basic 1-ton models,
- from PLN 24,309 net — 1.2-ton models,
- PLN 30,000–40,000 net — versions with a cabin or a Kubota engine.

This is a one-time cost that can also be spread out through installments or leasing.

## When Does a Mini Excavator Pay for Itself?

This is the most important question.

Let us assume a simple scenario:

- rental: an average of PLN 300 per day,
- you work: 15 days per month,
- monthly rental cost = PLN 4,500.

In that case, after 5 months you spend PLN 22,500, which is about the same as the cost of your own mini excavator.

In practice, this means that after one season the machine starts making money for itself. Every additional month after that is pure gain compared with renting.

## Renting vs Buying — Comparison

### Renting

Advantages:

- no large upfront expense,
- no service responsibility on your side.

Disadvantages:

- high long-term costs,
- limited availability during the season,
- dependence on rental companies.

### Buying

Disadvantage:

- upfront investment.

Advantages:

- full equipment availability,
- the ability to work whenever you want,
- no rental costs,
- the machine works for your profit.

## Mini Excavator Leasing — A Middle-Ground Solution

If you do not want to commit all your cash, you can use leasing.

In many cases, the leasing installment is similar to the cost of renting. The difference is that with leasing you build your own asset, while with renting the money simply disappears.

Additionally, leasing offers:

- tax benefits,
- the possibility to deduct VAT,
- equipment depreciation.

## Who Is Buying a Mini Excavator Worth It For?

Buying makes sense if:

- you carry out work regularly, even just a few days a month,
- you run a construction, paving, or installation business,
- you want to become independent from rental companies,
- you plan to grow your business.

If you use a mini excavator more than a few times a month, buying almost always wins.

## Summary — Is It Worth Buying a Mini Excavator?

Renting only makes sense for occasional work. If, however, you work regularly, your own mini excavator pays for itself very quickly and starts generating real profit.

That is why more and more companies are choosing to buy instead of rent.
